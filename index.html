What a photo can quietly reveal

  • GPS coordinates — the exact spot a picture was taken, down to a few meters. Your home, your kid's school.
  • Your device — make, model, lens, and serial-adjacent identifiers that link photos across the internet to one camera.
  • Timestamps — when you were where, in your local timezone.
  • Editing trail — the software you used and original capture data, even after you crop.

MetaStrip reads all of it locally, shows you the risk, and removes it losslessly — it surgically drops the metadata blocks without re-compressing your image, so quality is untouched.
